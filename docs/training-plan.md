# Training plan

Every goal is a feat with a ladder of milestones. Now feats get full training, Next feats unlock when their condition is met, and Background feats ride along in mobility work. Training runs on a high-low week: three hard days, three easy days and one recovery day.

This is the plan as of October 2026. Once the app is set up, the database is the source of truth and changes are recorded in its change log.

**Principles**

- **High-low:** jumps, sprints and heavy squats or deadlifts share days, so legs get a real recovery day between. No day is "medium hard" for the legs.
- **Session order:** free-movement warm-up, handstand, jumps or sprints, the heavy lift, accessories, running or core, then mobility.
- **Low-cost work runs every session:** mobility, handstand practice and movement flow barely touch recovery.
- **Capped work:** heavy lifts, max jumps, sprints and straight-arm skills. Add new ones only when something else drops to maintenance.
- **Stretch timing:** no deep static stretching within about an hour before jumps or sprints.
- **Sports:** basketball and hard Muay Thai rounds count as hard days. Table tennis, pickleball and Muay Thai technique work count as easy.

## Ladders

Move up a step after three straight sessions at target, and keep the same exercise until then. The **bold** step is the feat.

### Lifting: 315 squat, 315 bench, 405 deadlift

- **Weekly (from Oct 18, 2026):** bench Sun, Tue and Fri; squat Mon and Sat; deadlift Wed.
- **Ladder:** warm-ups 33% x 10 and 56% x 10, then 79% x 5, 86% x 3, 91% x 1, 96% x 1, 91% x 1, 91% x 1, stopping after the singles. Tuesday bench is the light day: warm-ups, then 3 singles at 91%, no 96%.
- **Progression:** after every heavy session the training max adjusts from how the 96% single felt (RPE logged per set): easy (RPE 7 or less) adds 5 lb to bench or 10 lb to squat and deadlift, solid (7.5–8.5) adds half that, hard (9–9.5) holds, and a max effort or miss drops half a step. The light Tuesday doesn't move it.
- **Milestones:** every 20 lb from the current max up to the target.

### Jumping: one-hand dunk, then two-hand dunk

- **Weekly:** max-intent approach jumps at the rim Mon, reactive jumps Sat.
- **Unlock:** two-hand work starts once the one-hand dunk is repeatable.

1. Grab the rim
2. Dunk a tennis ball
3. Dunk a volleyball
4. Dunk a size-6 ball
5. **One-hand dunk**
6. **Two-hand dunk** (needs a few more inches of clearance)

### Running: sub-6:00 mile, sub-24:00 5K

- **Weekly:** sprints Wed. Saturday intervals alternate weekly: odd weeks are mile work (5–6 x 400 m at 90 s, 2–3 min rest), even weeks are 5K work (4–5 x 1 km at 4:45, 2 min rest). Zone 2 run Sun, 45–60 min.
- **Milestones:** mile 6:45, 6:30, 6:15, then **sub-6:00**. 5K 25:30, 24:45, then **sub-24:00**. Time trials at week 12.

### Accessories: delts, arms and durability

- **Goal:** bigger side delts and arms, plus healthy shoulders, elbows, wrists and knees.
- **Weekly:** lateral raises, curls and triceps work Tue, Fri and Sun (about 9 hard sets each per week); rotator cuff and forearms Fri; band walks in hard-day warm-ups; neck Sun.
- **Progression:** stay in 10–15 reps. Add reps to the top of the range, then add weight. Swap variations every 6–12 weeks.

Weekday sessions in the evening, weekends whenever; every session ends with a 20-minute mobility finisher.

| Tue | Easy legs | Heavy bench, weighted pull-ups, front lever, rotational core, arms and delts | B: shoulders and spine |
| Wed | Hard | Sprints, heavy deadlift, posterior and adductor work | B |
| Thu | Recovery | Yin class or 45-min flexibility session | Yin or long holds |
| Fri | Easy legs | Bench volume, muscle-ups, handstand push-ups, planche prep, compression, arms and delts | A |
| Sat | Hard | Reactive jumps, backflip, intervals, split squats | A |

Playing basketball? Put it on a hard day, cutting that day's jump volume, and keep easy days easy.

**Time:** weekdays run about 1.5–2 hours including mobility. Saturday takes about 2 hours and Sunday about 1.5, leaving spare Saturday time for basketball or Muay Thai.

## Mobility routines

PNF means hold 20 s, contract against the floor for 5–10 s at about 50%, then relax deeper.

- **Warm-up, every session (3 min):** crawl, roll and flow between squat, 90/90, lunge and bridge with no plan. On hard days, add 2–3 min of jump rope and band walks, 2 x 15 steps per side.
- **Front split, every session (8 min):** PNF on both sides, 2–3 rounds each. Hips square, blocks under the hands.
- **Routine A, hips and hamstrings (12 min):** pike PNF 3 rounds, pancake PNF 3–4 rounds, rocking frog 90 s, middle split PNF against a wall or on sliders 3 rounds.
- **Routine B, shoulders, spine and hip rotation (12 min):** bridge holds 3 x 20–30 s (wall-walk down until a floor bridge comes), wall slides 10 plus chin tucks 10, reverse prayer 60 s (start with fists or forearms together), open book 8 per side, lotus prep 60 s per side. Any knee pain means stop.
- **Passive holds (Thu and Sun):** wall straddle 3–5 min, supported reclined hero on a bolster 2 min, relaxed seated forward fold 2 min.
- **Minimum version (10 min):** one front split PNF round per side, 2 pancake PNF rounds, 2 minutes of free movement. Use it on bad days instead of skipping.

## Session details

Each session runs 75–90 minutes plus the mobility finisher. Heavy lifts use the ascending ladder off the training max.

**Mon: hard (jumps and squat)**

1. Handstand practice, 10 min
2. Max-intent jumps, 8–12 contacts, full rest between
3. Squat, ascending ladder
4. Squat back-off sets, 3 x 5 at about 75%
5. Tib raises, hip flexor raises and bent-knee calf raises, 2 x 15 each
6. Pike compression lifts, 3 x 10

**Tue: easy legs (upper strength, core and arms)**

1. Handstand practice, 10 min
2. Front lever progression, 4–5 x 8–12 s holds, done fresh before pulling
3. Bench, ascending ladder
4. Weighted pull-ups, 4 x 3–5
5. Chest-supported rows, 3 x 8, and prone Y-T-W, 2 x 10
6. Med ball rotational throws, 3 x 5 per side, and Pallof press, 2 x 10 per side
7. Lateral raises, 3 x 12–15; incline curls, 3 x 10; overhead triceps extensions, 3 x 10

**Wed: hard (sprints and deadlift)**

1. Handstand practice, 10 min
2. Sprints: 4–6 x 20–30 m accelerations, then 2–3 x 20 m flying, full rest
3. Deadlift, ascending ladder
4. Nordic curls, 3 x 4–6
5. Hip thrusts, 3 x 8
6. Straddle good mornings (light), 3 x 8; Cossack squats, 2 x 6 per side; Copenhagen planks, 2 x 20 s per side

**Thu: recovery**

1. Yin class, or a 45-min session of long holds on split, pancake, pike, bridge and lotus prep
2. Foot work, 10 min, and rolling practice (forward and back rolls, then breakfalls), 5 min

**Fri: easy legs (upper volume and skills)**

1. Handstand practice, 15 min
2. Muscle-ups, 4 x 3, crisp reps only
3. Handstand push-up progression, 3 x 5–8
4. Bench volume, 4 x 6–8 at about 70–75%
5. Wrist prep, 5 min, then planche leans, 3 x 20–30 s (from week 7)
6. Pike pulses, 3 x 10, and tuck L-sit, 3 x 15 s
7. Face pulls, 2 x 12, and suitcase carries, 3 x 30 m per side
8. Lateral raises, 3 x 15; hammer curls, 3 x 10; triceps pushdowns, 3 x 12
9. External rotations, 2 x 15; wrist curls and reverse curls, 2 x 15 each

**Sat: hard (reactive jumps, backflip and intervals)**

1. Handstand practice, 10 min
2. Reactive jumps (pogos, hurdle hops, bounds, lateral bounds), 15–20 contacts, then backflip progression, 15 min
3. Intervals, alternating weekly: mile week 5–6 x 400 m at 90 s (10 mph on a treadmill), 2–3 min rest; 5K week 4–5 x 1 km at 4:45, 2 min rest. Add reps first, then shorten rest.
4. ATG split squats, 3 x 6–8 per side
5. Foot work, 10 min

**Sun: easy (aerobic, arms and flexibility)**

- Zone 2 run, 45–60 min, then 30 min of long holds on split, pancake and bridge
- Arms and delts, 20 min: handstand push-up progression, 3 x 5–8, then lateral raises, curls and triceps extensions, 3 x 15 each, plus light neck work, 2 x 15

## 12-week cycle

Advance by benchmarks, not dates. If a gate check fails, repeat the previous phase rather than pushing ahead.

1. **Weeks 1–5, foundation:** all sessions as written; every skill starts at step 1 or 2. Mobility finisher every session; front lever once a week.
2. **Week 6, deload and first test.** Pass: mobility done in most sessions, elbows and knees pain-free, main lifts still progressing. Not yet? Repeat weeks 1–5.
3. **Weeks 7–11, load and expand:** start planche leans; Next feats unlock as their conditions are met. Front lever twice a week if elbows allow; add interval reps.
4. **Week 12, deload and full retest:** retest everything, including the mile and 5K time trials. Mark achieved feats and promote Next feats whose unlocks are met.
5. **Week 13 on, next cycle:** achieved feats drop to maintenance. Next up: two-hand dunk, press handstand, planche, one-arm pull-up.

**Test day (weeks 6 and 12, then every 5–6 weeks)**

| Test | How |
| --- | --- |
| Splits, pancake and bridge | Side-view photo at the same leg width each time |
| Handstand | Longest freestanding hold, or current ladder step |
| Calisthenics feats | Current ladder step |
| Vertical | Max jump touch height |
| Lifts | Training max progression; no separate 1RM test |
| Mile and 5K | Time trials at week 12, a few days apart |

## Adjusting

Cut work when two or more of these last over a week: several lifts or skills stalling, joints nagging, sleep or mood dipping, or sessions getting skipped.

**Cut in this order**

1. Front lever and planche work, down to one session at half the sets
2. Saturday reactive jumps, halved
3. Saturday intervals, swapped for an easy Zone 2 run
4. Accessories on hard days, down to the main lift plus one

**Never cut:** the main lifts, Monday jumps, or the mobility finisher (use the minimum version).

**Deload** every 5–6 weeks: halve the sets, keep weights moderate, and keep mobility and handstand practice as normal.

**Warning signs to stop and back off**

- Inner-elbow or biceps-tendon pain: pause straight-arm work for a week.
- Inner or outer knee pain in lotus or hero: back off to the previous step.
- Achilles or calf soreness that lingers into a hard day: cut that day's jump contacts in half.
